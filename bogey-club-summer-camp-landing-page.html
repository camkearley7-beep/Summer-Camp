Camp Information

Everything parents need to know.

Here are the key details for the week so you know exactly what to expect before registering your child.

  • The Bogey Club is located at 17705 Leslie Street, Unit 4 & 5, Newmarket, Ontario.
  • Children between 8 years old and 15 years old can register. No golf experience required.
  • Each day, campers receive golf instruction and game play.
  • All golf instruction will be led by Bogey Club teaching professionals.
  • Campers can bring their own golf clubs or let the team know if they will require rentals for the week. The Bogey Club has a limited number of rental sets. Please email jake@thebogeyclub.com to set up rental clubs.
  • Campers must provide their own lunch throughout the week. Pizza is available for purchase in store.
  • Camp space is limited to 16 participants per week max.
  • There will be 3-4 Golf & Camp Counselors.

Parent Questions

Everything you need to know.

My kid has never played golf. Is that okay?
Totally okay - most of our campers are beginners.
Is camp really 100% indoors?
Yes. Every single session happens inside our air-conditioned Newmarket facility on Golfzon simulators.
What should my kid bring?
A packed lunch, a refillable water bottle, and golf clubs if you have them. Rentals are available.
How old does my child need to be?
Campers aged 8 to 15 are welcome.
Is it safe? Who's supervising?
Every week has trained golf and camp counselors, with instruction led by Bogey Club teaching pros.
Do you offer discounts for siblings or multiple weeks?
Yes - 10% off for each sibling and 10% off for multi-week bookings.
What's the cancellation policy?
The $150 deposit is non-refundable. Contact us to discuss transfers based on availability.
Where are you located?
17705 Leslie Street, Units 4 and 5, Newmarket, Ontario.
